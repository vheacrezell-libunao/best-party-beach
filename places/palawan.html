PALAWAN

Puerto Princesa, Palawan

Palawan is one of the most beautiful places in the Philippines. It is known for its clear blue water, beautiful beaches, limestone cliffs, and rich natural environment.

Visitors can enjoy swimming, island hopping, sightseeing, and exploring nature. Palawan is also a great place to relax and spend time with family and friends.
