Intramuros

Intramuros is a historic district in Manila known for its old stone walls, churches, buildings, and historical landmarks. I recommend this place because it is a great place to learn about Philippine history and culture. Visitors can walk around the area and appreciate its unique architecture and historical atmosphere.

Address Intramuros, Manila, Philippines
Opening Hours Varies by attraction
Price Range Free to paid attractions
Best Time to Visit Morning or late afternoon
