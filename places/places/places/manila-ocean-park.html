Manila Ocean Park

Manila Ocean Park is a marine-themed attraction in Manila. Visitors can see different kinds of marine animals and enjoy various activities. It is a fun place to learn more about marine life while spending time together with family and friends.

Address Manila, Philippines
Opening Hours Varies by schedule
Price Range Paid admission
Best Time to Visit Weekdays or early in the day
